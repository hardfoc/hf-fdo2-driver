Hardware¶

What to connect between your MCU and an FDO2-G2, with which UART settings, and the timing and power the module needs.

All values here come from the FDO2-G2 datasheet (document version v5, 03/2026): specifications in section 2, the interface in section 4, the connector in section 7.

Connector¶

The module has a Molex 560020-0420 socket (four positions) on its back, next to the venting capillary. Directions are seen from the sensor.

Pin Name Function
1 GND Ground
2 TXD Output: UART transmit, 3.0 V levels
3 RXD Input: UART receive, 3.0 V levels, 3.3 V tolerant
4 VCC Supply, 3.3 V to 5.0 V DC

Connect MCU TX to pin 3 and MCU RX to pin 2. There are no other control pins: no reset, enable or interrupt line. The module is always on while VCC is present.

3.3 V maximum on RXD

The UART runs at 3.0 V and its absolute maximum is 3.3 V. A 3.3 V MCU can drive RXD directly; a 5 V MCU needs a level shifter or a divider on its TX line. The 3.0 V high level on TXD is above the input-high threshold of most 3.3 V MCUs; check yours.

UART settings¶

Setting Value
Baud rate 19200 after every power-up (fdo2::kFdo2G2DefaultBaud)
Frame 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit
Flow control None
Command end CR (0x0D), or CR LF
Reply end CR only
Other rates 1200 to 115200 with #BAUD, until the next power cycle; the driver does not send it

The datasheet recommends 19200 for the highest reliability. At 19200 baud a #MOXY reply (about 25 characters) takes about 13 ms on the wire.

Timing¶

Event Time
Power-up to first command about 1 s (kFdo2G2PowerUpSettleMs = 1100 ms)
Measurement, M = 0 to 2 (factory: 2) up to about 150 ms
Measurement, M = 3 or 4 up to about 300 ms
#CALO or #CAHI calibration up to 10 s
Response time t63 under 2 s
Warm-up to full accuracy 3 min

M is the flash duration mode set with #SETM (section 4.3); response time and warm-up are from section 2.

The module measures only when asked (unless broadcast mode is on), so the measurement time is part of every #MOXY and #MRAW transaction:

sequenceDiagram
    participant H as Host (driver)
    participant S as FDO2-G2
    H->>H: flush_rx()
    H->>S: MOXY command, CR
    Note over S: temperature, ambient light, oxygen, housing sensors (up to 150 ms at M = 2)
    S-->>H: MOXY 203456 17892 0, CR
    H->>H: parse, scale: 203.456 hPa, 17.892 C, status 0

The driver's default measurement timeout is 250 ms. Raise it with SetMeasureTimeoutMs() if the module is set to M = 3 or 4 (Configuration).

Power¶

Parameter Value
Supply voltage 3.3 V to 5.0 V DC
Absolute maximum supply 5.5 V DC
Current, standby / peak about 8 mA / 40 mA
Energy per measurement about 1 to 2 mAs

Do not interrupt the supply while the module writes its flash (after #CALO, #CAHI, #CRCE, #SETM or #BCST): the datasheet warns that the flash can be corrupted. The driver sends none of these commands.

Environment and mounting¶

Parameter Value
Operating temperature −10 °C to 60 °C
Storage temperature −40 °C to 60 °C
Internal pressure sensor range 300 mbar to 1100 mbar
Maximum absolute / differential pressure 20 bar / 3 bar
Humidity back side non-condensing; no dew point inside the membrane
Mounting M16 x 1 thread; Ø 28.5 mm x 28 mm; 10.5 g

Keep direct sunlight and strong lamps off the sensing membrane: ambient light shows up in the status word and in the #MRAW ambient-light field. The cross-sensitivity table (section 3) lists the gases that disturb or damage the sensor, NO2 and Cl2 among them.

Errata¶

Erratum Affects Status
Increased noise at two module-specific temperatures, typically between 8 °C and 15 °C, each about ±0.1 °C wide Firmware 3.55 Fixed in firmware 3.57 with a hardware change (PCN-FDO2-G2-001)

ReadVersion() returns the firmware revision: firmware_revision 357 is 3.57.

Evaluation¶

PyroScience sells the module with a USB adapter cable and the "Simple FDO2 Logger" software for Windows, which installs a virtual COM port to the module's UART. It is a quick way to see the module answer before you wire it to an MCU. The ESP32 examples run on an ESP32-S3 with the module on UART1.
